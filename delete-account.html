Request Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Veylora account and associated data.

Account deletion is permanent. Before you request deletion, export or save any invoices, quotations, or other information you need to keep. We cannot restore your account or cloud data after deletion is completed.

Request Account Deletion

Or email: sherazhussain360@gmail.com

For security, please send the request from the email address associated with your Veylora account whenever possible.

Do not send:

  • Passwords
  • OTP codes
  • Authentication codes
  • Payment card information
  • Firebase tokens
  • Other credentials

Sending a request by email does not by itself mean your account has already been deleted. We will process verified requests in line with our Privacy Policy.

What happens to your data?

When your account is deleted, we remove your associated Veylora cloud data, including where applicable:

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • Business profile
  • Customers
  • Products and services
  • Quotations
  • Invoices
  • Payment records
  • User settings
  • Invoice and quotation template preferences
  • Business logo and related cloud storage data
  • Other Veylora cloud data associated with your account

Local data stored on your device is cleared when you complete account deletion in the Veylora app (see below).

Data Retention

Some information may be retained where required or permitted for legitimate legal requirements, security, fraud prevention, or compliance purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.

As stated in our Privacy Policy, feedback you have submitted may be retained for up to 24 months for product improvement, then deleted or anonymized where practicable. Feedback may remain associated with your user ID after account deletion because the app cannot remove all feedback records through the client application alone.

Delete Your Account From the Veylora App

You can also delete your Veylora account directly from the Veylora app.

Path: More → Delete account

You may need to sign in again (for example, with your password, Google, or Apple) before deletion completes, depending on how you signed up.

Uninstalling the Veylora app does not delete your cloud-stored Veylora account or associated data.

Account Verification

For security, Veylora may need to verify that you are authorized to request deletion of the account.

Please send your request from the email address associated with your Veylora account whenever possible. Never send passwords or authentication codes by email.
