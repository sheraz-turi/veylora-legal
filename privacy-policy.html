1. Introduction

Welcome to Veylora – Invoice & Quotation Maker (“Veylora,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). Veylora helps freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small businesses create and manage quotations, invoices, customers, products, and payment records.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following categories of information:

Account information

  • Email address
  • Password (stored and handled by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your password in plain text)
  • Display name, if you provide one when registering
  • Authentication and account identifiers (such as your Firebase user ID)

Business information

  • Business name, address, and contact details you enter
  • Business logo, if you upload one
  • Tax and payment-related business settings you configure
  • Invoice and quote settings and PDF template preferences

Customer and document information

  • Customer names, contact details, and addresses
  • Products and services
  • Quotation and invoice details (line items, prices, taxes, discounts, totals)
  • Payment records linked to invoices
  • Notes and payment terms

You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate legal basis or permission to enter and process information belonging to your customers and other third parties.

Support and feedback

If you use in-app support or feedback, we may collect:

  • Your user ID
  • Feedback category and message text
  • Optional star rating
  • App version and build number, device platform, operating system version, app language/locale, theme preference, and the screen you were on when submitting

Feedback is used to improve Veylora. It is not intended to include your customer names, invoice contents, or other business document details unless you choose to type them into the feedback message.

Usage analytics

When Firebase is available, we use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. We log event names and limited, non-content parameters (for example, feature categories or screen identifiers). We do not send customer names, invoice or quote contents, email addresses, phone numbers, or financial amounts to analytics.

Local device storage

Veylora stores a copy of your business data in a local database on your device so the app works offline. Business logos may also be cached locally for PDF generation. This local data is cleared when you delete your account through the app.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Veylora’s features
  • Authenticate you and protect your account
  • Save and synchronize your business data across your devices when you are signed in
  • Generate PDF quotations and invoices on your device
  • Respond to support requests and review feedback
  • Measure aggregate app usage and improve reliability and usability
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues

We do not use your invoice, quotation, or customer data for unrelated marketing purposes.

Subscriptions: The current public version of Veylora is offered without in-app subscriptions. If we introduce optional paid features in the future through the Apple App Store or Google Play, those purchases will be processed by the respective store; we will update this policy to describe any additional data processed for billing and entitlements.

4. Data storage and third-party services

Veylora uses third-party services operated by Google (Firebase), including:

  • Firebase Authentication – account sign-in and account management
  • Cloud Firestore – cloud storage and synchronization of your structured app data (business profile, customers, products, quotes, invoices, payments, settings, and templates)
  • Firebase Storage – storage of business logo images you upload
  • Firebase Analytics – aggregated usage analytics as described above

Optional feedback submissions are stored in a separate Firestore collection used for product improvement.

Apple App Store and Google Play distribute the app. Their privacy practices apply to downloads, updates, and any store-account or purchase data they process independently of us.

We do not store generated PDF files in Firebase Firestore. PDFs are generated on your device and may be shared through your device’s share sheet to apps you choose.

Third-party providers process data under their own privacy policies and applicable agreements. See Google Firebase Privacy.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including access controls on cloud data tied to your account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

  • Account and business data: Retained while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your associated cloud data under your user account, your authentication account, your uploaded logo in Storage (where deletion succeeds), and local app data on that device, subject to legal or security retention requirements.
  • Feedback: Retained for up to 24 months for product improvement, then deleted or anonymized where practicable. Feedback may remain associated with your user ID after account deletion because the app cannot remove feedback records from our systems through the client application alone.
  • Analytics: Retained according to retention settings configured in Firebase Analytics (see Google’s documentation).

7. Account and data deletion

You can delete your Veylora account from the app (More → Delete account). You will be asked to confirm with your password. Deleting the app from your device does not delete cloud-stored data.

You may also contact us at sherazhussain360@gmail.com for help with access, correction, or deletion requests.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the email above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children’s privacy

Veylora is intended for business use and is not directed at children below the age at which consent is required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. International transfers

Firebase services may process and store data in countries where Google operates data centers. By using Veylora, you acknowledge that your information may be processed in those locations subject to applicable safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Your continued use of Veylora after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
